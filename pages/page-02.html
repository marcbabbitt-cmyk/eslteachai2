Tenses Overview

A solid understanding of English tenses is crucial for FCE preparation. This section covers the most important tenses you'll need to master.

Tense Formation Usage
Present Simple Subject + Base Verb Habits, facts, routines
Present Continuous am/is/are + -ing Actions happening now
Past Simple Subject + Past Verb Completed actions

Common Errors

Tip: Pay attention to subject-verb agreement. The verb must match the subject in number and person.

Many learners struggle with the correct use of present perfect versus past simple. Remember: present perfect connects the past to the present.
