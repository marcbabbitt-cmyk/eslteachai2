Vocabulary Building

Expand your lexical range for advanced English communication

Essential Vocabulary Categories

Academic and Professional Terms
  • Analyze - to examine something in detail
  • Implement - to put a plan into action
  • Evaluate - to assess the value or quality
  • Comprehend - to understand fully

Word Families

Learning words in families helps you expand your vocabulary efficiently. Words that share the same root often have related meanings.
Example:
Noun: Innovation
Verb: Innovate
Adjective: Innovative
Adverb: Innovatively

Collocations

Collocations are combinations of words that commonly go together. Mastering collocations will make your English sound more natural.

  • Make a decision
  • Take responsibility
  • Reach an agreement
